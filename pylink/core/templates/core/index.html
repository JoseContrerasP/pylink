How does it work?

  • Make your URL shorter

    PyLink generates a 7-character code made up of numbers and letters. You can also generate a QR code and share your shortened URLs on major social media.

  • Manage your links easily

    When you sign up, every shortened URL is stored in your personal dashboard. From there, you can copy, edit, delete, or search for any link you’ve created, keeping everything organized and easy to access.

  • Keep everything in one place

    Your saved links stay connected to your account, so you can return anytime and manage them without hassle. Whether you’re on desktop or mobile, your URLs are always available when you need them.
